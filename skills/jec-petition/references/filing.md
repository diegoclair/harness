# Filing

Channels and limits change by court and by year. Confirm everything below on the court's own site in
the current session; the page itself may be stale (in the original case the court's page still showed
the cap computed with a minimum wage three years old).

## Before filing

- **Competent court.** Use the court's territorial-competence lookup with the plaintiff's postal code.
  Do not infer the forum from the neighbourhood name.
- **Cap.** Without a lawyer the claim must stay under the lower statutory cap, computed with the
  minimum wage in force. With a lawyer the cap is higher and electronic filing is direct.
- **Who signs.** Every plaintiff signs the petition and any declaration.
- **Complete package.** Petition, exhibits in the numbered order, IDs, proof of address.

## How people without a lawyer file

State courts typically offer some of these; check which exist:

- A form plus the petition and documents sent by e-mail to the competent small-claims court. Some
  courts ask the plaintiff to confirm first, through a virtual help desk or by phone, that the unit
  accepts e-mail filing.
- Electronic filing by the plaintiff with a personal digital certificate.
- In person at the court's intake desk, where a clerk reduces the claim to writing.

Give the user the page, the form link and the address list you found, and the single next step. Do not
send anything on their behalf.

## What to tell the plaintiffs

- The injunction request is usually decided within days, without them doing anything.
- There will be a conciliation hearing, often by video. **Each plaintiff must attend; absence ends the
  case for the absent one.**
- The defendant frequently offers a settlement at that hearing. Decide beforehand the minimum that
  would be accepted, and note that a settlement with broad release can extinguish older claims not yet
  filed — a reason to keep a connected older claim in the same action.
- First-instance small claims carry no court costs or fee awards; an appeal requires a lawyer and
  costs.
- Witnesses listed in the petition come to the hearing without being summoned; warn them.
- Keep collecting: the next invoice may bring protocol numbers and daily usage that show the outage
  days.

## After filing

Record in the case file: the case number, the court unit, the date, and every deadline the court sets.
If the judge questions the joinder of an older claim, withdraw that request alone and refile it before
the most conservative limitation date already computed.
