# Which technique, for a brand nobody knows

Read this when choosing what a piece applies. The evidence, numbers and sources live in the project's research; this
is the judgment. In a project with a sales research file, open it first and let it win.

| Technique | Use when | Honest form | Fails when |
|---|---|---|---|
| The reader's own data | the product can show something of theirs for free | "see it before paying" | it promises a finding not everyone has |
| Peer voice | a real customer agrees | their words, their name, with consent | invented, or a low count shown as proof |
| Risk reversal | any first purchase | free trial, no card, refund, what stays in their control | it hides what does change |
| Real deadline | the owner will honour it | a date or "limited time", kept | a date planned to be false, a timer that resets |
| Founder's face | the objection is "who is this" | a person who truly answers | a promise of access nobody keeps |
| Admitting a limit | the reader is skeptical | one true limit, stated plainly | it creates a doubt they did not have |
| Price by the day | the monthly number scares | the daily figure beside the real price and what comes after | it hides the later price, or reads as a fee per item |
| Loss in money | a measured loss of theirs exists | their own number | a generic scare |
| A second benefit | it stacks on the first | shown as a step of the first offer | shown as a rival number |

## Rules of use

- A discount does not open a cold reader in a season full of discounts. Lead with who and what, or with the discount only
  when the subject also names the thing.
- Show the full price, the offer price and the price after. Distrust of seasonal discounts is the audience's default.
- One call to action per message. A second ask goes in a line after it, or waits for its own message.
- A channel changes the piece: read the marketing rules of the channel before adapting, and never paste one channel's text
  into another. A group post is once per group, so it goes out at its best.
