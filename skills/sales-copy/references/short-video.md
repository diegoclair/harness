# A short video that a stranger finishes: Reel, Short, video ad

Read this before writing the speech of a vertical video, and before choosing what goes on its screen. The hook itself
comes from `hooks.md`.

## The shape

A short video is decided in the first two seconds and kept by the next five.

| When | Part | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | the hook | the claim, spoken and on screen, written apart. Motion in the first frame, never a still logo |
| 2–7 s | the stake | why it matters to the person watching, in one line |
| then | the body | one idea per beat, and the frame changes every beat; a still shot past ~4 s is where people leave |
| last 3 s | the payoff | deliver what the hook promised, then the single ask |
| last line | the loop | echo a word of the hook, so the replay lands clean |

- **15 to 45 seconds.** Under 7 s inflates loops and nothing else; past 60 s only a story holds.
- **One idea per video.** A script with two ideas is two videos.
- **Cut the intro.** No greeting, no name, no logo sting. The video starts at the sentence that would normally come
  at second six.
- **Numbers beat adjectives, and only real ones.** Ask for the number before writing around the hole.

## The screen is a second script

It is read before it is heard: most people start muted.

- **Six words or fewer per card**, the hook card up at frame 1.
- **The safe zone on 1080×1920:** nothing that must be read above y≈230 or below y≈1440, and the right ~230 px clear.
  The caption, the action rail and the audio strip cover the rest. Burned-in subtitles that sit below 1440 are hidden
  under the post's own caption; a story's reply bar covers below ~1600.
- **Burn in subtitles for the body**, inside that box.
- **The cover is chosen, never the first frame by accident.** On a channel that takes the thumbnail from the first
  frame (a WhatsApp file), the cut opens on the cover frame.

## Test hooks, not whole videos

When money buys the reach, write three to five hooks from different formulas over the same body, and let the
platform's 3-second hold choose. A new body is only worth shooting once a hook holds. Text cannot predict which of two
decent hooks travels: face, edit, audio and distribution decide that, so the retention graph outranks any score.

## Before it goes out

- Read the speech out loud at the real pace: any line that needs rehearsing is rewritten in spoken Portuguese.
- Run the tells check in `ai-tells.md` on the speech and the caption.
- Name the formula used beside the post's record, so the audit (`research.md`) can tell later which shape worked.
