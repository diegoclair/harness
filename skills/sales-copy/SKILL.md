# sales-copy

Born from one e-mail in Oct/Nov 2026: seven versions were rejected before one shipped. Agents wrote cleverly, a
lone specialist wrote cleverly with sources, and a critic simulating a seller liked a line the founder rejected in
seconds. Each rule below quotes the complaint that produced it, so a familiar complaint leads straight to its fix.

## The order

1. **Read the product's rulers first**: what may be promised, how the brand writes, the offer as it stands. They own
   the facts; this skill owns the method. Check every claim against the code or the owner doc on the day it goes out.
2. **Ask for the human's taste before writing, when it is missing.** A real e-mail he would open, or a one-minute audio
   of how he tells a friend. No amount of agents replaces this. "Ninguém consegue construir um e-mail que faz sentido"
   came after four rounds with no reference.
3. **Build the piece, not only the words, for an offer.** Plain text hid the emotion: "pode ser que você me passando só
   o texto, não tá tendo emoção". An offer is seen before it is read, so the image or video is chosen first.
4. **Run the team on anything that reaches a customer** (below), and bring the human one version with what was weighed.

## What failed, and the rule

| Complaint | Rejected | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| "a pessoa nem sabe direito quem é" | "achei um problema, corrigi" | The reader does not know you. Your news, your bug and your story are not the opening |
| "nem eu tô com vontade de ler" | "você criou sua conta e agora ela vale metade" | Do not open on your own event |
| "as coisas nem se linkaram" | a clever quote, then features, then the discount | One thread from subject to button. Whatever opens must be the reason for what follows; a reader of the first and last line alone gets who, what and why now |
| "ninguém esperou" | "a espera acabou" | Never state a past the reader did not live |
| "qual conta? do banco?" | "o que está travado na sua conta" | Name the thing. A bare noun means something to the writer only |
| "assumindo que tem algo" | a subject presuming a problem | Do not assert the state of the reader's business |
| "parece que tá falando de um robô" | "e amanhã tudo de novo" | No line of effect. If it could end a TV ad, cut it |
| "pode desmerecer o trabalho" | "virou segundo emprego" | Never suppose the size or weight of the reader's work. It may be their whole income |
| "soando arrogante" | "eu desligo quando decidir" | Aggressive means technique, never tone. Power over the reader reads as contempt |
| "parece que quebrou o contexto" | a big R$ 40 right after a big 50% | Two numbers side by side look like two offers. Show the second as a step of the first |
| "precisa de tudo isso?" | a legal footer in full | Keep what a rule demands, cut the rest, and say which rule |
| "limitando as pessoas até mil pedidos" | a post opening on "vende até 1.000 pedidos/mês?" | The target order band guides the brief, never the opening. Naming a ceiling tells everyone else it is not for them |
| "acima dos concorrentes de 59, 69" | a post opening on the top tier and R$ 199 | Open on the lowest honest price or on no price. The tier table belongs at the close, once the reader wants it |
| "não é chamativo, não é energizado" | a correct, flat line of offer and tier | A correct opening can still be dead. A post must make someone want to go and see, with energy and a promise of where this is going |
| "a imagem/vídeo pode apelar" | a finished text with no image or video chosen | Choose the image or video before the words. What the media says, the text does not repeat |
| "é só imprimir etiqueta" | a 25 s cut with 14 s on one feature | A piece about the product shows its range in fast cuts. One feature gets a video of its own, and the differential opens |
| "omite o que não precisa e coloca em evidência o que ajuda" | "minutos depois, o aviso chega" | Do not volunteer a harmless limit. Say the true gain and leave out the delay, without claiming what is false |
| "começar pedindo ajuda não é legal, lá é um grupo de vendas" | a sales post opening on "preciso da sua ajuda" | In a selling venue, open by offering. A favour is asked at the end, as one small step such as following a profile |
| "não podemos limitar a essas duas" | "a melhor plataforma pra quem vende no Mercado Livre e na Shopee" | The promise of where the brand is going names no current limit. What works today is said apart, as today's fact |
| "parece que tô falando com um investidor" | a plain e-mail opening on "aqui é o Diego, fundador" | Each e-mail is a built piece in the campaign's energy, never a formal note. The gift is news, not a request: say it is already theirs |
| "a fala que tem que ser impactante" | a cut with music and on-screen text only | A video sells by what is said: the real step-by-step of the seller's pain, in his words. Write the speech first and cut to it |
| "muita info e pouco sem tocar no problema", "não dá o tom de alerta" | a clever "venda que você paga pra fazer", then paragraphs of product facts | A serious pain is named in plain words on the first line, in the tone of an alert. A caption stays on the problem and the fix, and may retell the video's pain; facts that do not serve them are cut |
| "se tiver conectado em 50, você não colocaria os 50 na frase" | "confere suas vendas no Mercado Livre e na Shopee" | Say "no marketplace". A list of integrations reads as the limit and ages when the next one enters. Name one only when it is the subject of the piece |
| "imaginando um vendedor lendo esse slide, ele não vai entender nada" | "2020, um bot pro teste de release" | A founder's history is told by what each tool spared people, in the reader's words. Job jargon (stack, release, dev) means nothing outside the trade |
| "pra que colocar o valor de depois?!" | "a partir de R$ 49,50 por mês e de R$ 99 depois" | A post gives the discount, how many months and the offer price, and stops there. The later price lives on the page the reader lands on |

## What worked

Pieces the human approved at once, and why. Start from these before writing new.

- **The group post:** a loud first line in capitals, the offer, the promise of where the brand is going, the money that can
  reach zero, one link, and a small ask to follow at the end.
- **The two e-mails:** one big fact opens as a built piece, a personal line names the reader, the button comes before the
  explanation, and the referral block shows the fee reaching zero.
- **A gift is already given:** "já tá liberado na sua conta" beat asking them to reply.
- **A reader who asked gets a sentence first:** "a Rednev abriu" before the offer.
- **In the brand's profile the voice is "a gente"; the founder's "eu" is only for the founder's own post.**

Clarity wins over creativity in a subject line: say the offer plainly, specifically, with the main thing first, since
the phone cuts the end. A group post competes with suppliers shouting, so it also has to spark interest.

## Aggressive means technique

Read `references/techniques.md` when choosing what to apply. In short, for a brand nobody knows:

- The reader's own data beats a promise. Show them something of theirs before asking for money.
- Remove risk before the price: free trial, no card, what cannot happen without their yes.
- A peer's voice is the strongest proof, and it must exist. No payers means no testimonial and no counts.
- A founder with a face and an open door answers "who is this". Keep it only if they truly answer.
- One technique per line, never stacked: a heap reads as a scam.

## The line that does not move

Truth is the lock on every technique. No invented scarcity: "vaga", counters and fake deadlines are out where no limit
exists, and "limited time" is fine when the owner will really end it. An intended date that is later extended is not a
lie; a date planned to be false is. Never write what the system does not do: "só lê" was false, and only a critic reading
the decision doc caught it. Do not relitigate the human's commercial call once truth is safe: "foco é vender".

## Short video, Instagram and what already works

A video or a post is decided in its first three seconds, by someone who was not looking for it.

- Read `references/hooks.md` before writing any opening a stranger must stop for: three formulas, spoken and on-screen
  lines apart, never "eu" in the brand's voice, never a number that does not exist.
- Read `references/short-video.md` before writing the speech of a vertical video or choosing what goes on its screen.
- Hand the approved speech to the `video-production` skill before any voice is generated or a video rendered: it owns the narration, the scene timing, frame 0 and the reviews.
- Read `references/instagram.md` when the piece goes to Instagram: the caption's job, stories, replies, the profile.
- Read `references/research.md` before a video or an ad with no evidence of what works for this audience, and after
  posts have run: rank by the outlier multiple, never by raw views.
- Run `python3 scripts/tells.py <draft>` and read `references/ai-tells.md` before the human sees any caption, speech or
  post. A FAIL is fixed; a WARN is decided out loud.

## The team

Read `references/team.md` before dispatching. Two writers on opposite lines, two cold critics, one rewrite, one more cold
read. A simulated reader finds where attention dies; it is not a real person, so never report its reaction as evidence
from the market. When the human rejects a line, its reason goes into the brief before the next round.

## Keep it growing

Every rejection from the human becomes a row in the table above, with their words, in the same session. A rule with no
real complaint behind it does not enter: that is style opinion.

Revalidate when: a text that passed this method is rejected for a reason the table does not name, or a measured campaign contradicts a rule.
