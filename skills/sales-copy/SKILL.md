# sales-copy

Born from one e-mail in Oct/Nov 2026: seven versions were rejected before one shipped. Agents wrote cleverly, a
lone specialist wrote cleverly with sources, and a critic simulating a seller liked a line the founder rejected in
seconds. Each rule below quotes the complaint that produced it, so a familiar complaint leads straight to its fix.

## The order

1. **Read the product's rulers first**: what may be promised, how the brand writes, the offer as it stands. They own
   the facts; this skill owns the method. Check every claim against the code or the owner doc on the day it goes out.
2. **Ask for the human's taste before writing, when it is missing.** A real e-mail he would open, or a one-minute audio
   of how he tells a friend. No amount of agents replaces this. "Ninguém consegue construir um e-mail que faz sentido"
   came after four rounds with no reference.
3. **Build the piece, not only the words, for an offer.** Plain text hid the emotion: "pode ser que você me passando só
   o texto, não tá tendo emoção". An offer is seen before it is read.
4. **Run the team on anything that reaches a customer** (below), and bring the human one version with what was weighed.

## What failed, and the rule

| Complaint | Rejected | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| "a pessoa nem sabe direito quem é" | "achei um problema, corrigi" | The reader does not know you. Your news, your bug and your story are not the opening |
| "nem eu tô com vontade de ler" | "você criou sua conta e agora ela vale metade" | Do not open on your own event |
| "as coisas nem se linkaram" | a clever quote, then features, then the discount | One thread from subject to button. Whatever opens must be the reason for what follows; a reader of the first and last line alone gets who, what and why now |
| "ninguém esperou" | "a espera acabou" | Never state a past the reader did not live |
| "qual conta? do banco?" | "o que está travado na sua conta" | Name the thing. A bare noun means something to the writer only |
| "assumindo que tem algo" | a subject presuming a problem | Do not assert the state of the reader's business |
| "parece que tá falando de um robô" | "e amanhã tudo de novo" | No line of effect. If it could end a TV ad, cut it |
| "pode desmerecer o trabalho" | "virou segundo emprego" | Never suppose the size or weight of the reader's work. It may be their whole income |
| "soando arrogante" | "eu desligo quando decidir" | Aggressive means technique, never tone. Power over the reader reads as contempt |
| "parece que quebrou o contexto" | a big R$ 40 right after a big 50% | Two numbers side by side look like two offers. Show the second as a step of the first |
| "precisa de tudo isso?" | a legal footer in full | Keep what a rule demands, cut the rest, and say which rule |

Clarity wins over creativity. Published subject guides and this case agree: say the offer plainly, specifically, with
the main thing first, since the phone cuts the end. Curiosity from a stranger reads as odd.

## Aggressive means technique

Read `references/techniques.md` when choosing what to apply. In short, for a brand nobody knows:

- The reader's own data beats a promise. Show them something of theirs before asking for money.
- Remove risk before the price: free trial, no card, what cannot happen without their yes.
- A peer's voice is the strongest proof, and it must exist. No payers means no testimonial and no counts.
- A founder with a face and an open door answers "who is this". Keep it only if they truly answer.
- One technique per line, never stacked: a heap reads as a scam.

## The line that does not move

Truth is the lock on every technique. No invented scarcity: "vaga", counters and fake deadlines are out where no limit
exists, and "limited time" is fine when the owner will really end it. An intended date that is later extended is not a
lie; a date planned to be false is. Never write what the system does not do: "só lê" was false, and only a critic reading
the decision doc caught it. Do not relitigate the human's commercial call once truth is safe: "foco é vender".

## The team

Read `references/team.md` before dispatching. Two writers on opposite lines, two cold critics, one rewrite, one more cold
read. A simulated reader finds where attention dies; it is not a real person, so never report its reaction as evidence
from the market. When the human rejects a line, its reason goes into the brief before the next round.

## Keep it growing

Every rejection from the human becomes a row in the table above, with their words, in the same session. A rule with no
real complaint behind it does not enter: that is style opinion.

Revalidate when: a text that passed this method is rejected for a reason the table does not name, or a measured campaign contradicts a rule.
